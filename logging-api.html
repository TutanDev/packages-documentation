LogArchive class

A session's records, held after the store that wrote them has moved on. A console sink keeps one for its session, and LogFile.Read makes one from a file. Derives from LogRecords.

prop
int Count
How many records it holds.
prop
long Dropped
How many of its oldest it let go to stay under its cap, since it was last cleared.
method
LogErrorsHeld ErrorsFrom(long sequence)
The errors it holds from a sequence on, and where it is now, read at once, without allocating.

An error is an event's record at Error or Critical that was not written as an error's backlog. An error it let go, past its cap or on a clear, is not held, so it is not counted.

A console's Clear lets every record go: First and Next are then where it stopped, and it takes no record older. A record older than what it let go is let go too, and so is one older than all it holds while it is full. A file's archive, and an ended session's, hold what they held, and take nothing after.
